- Voice chat for PC VR games such as VRChat: the headset microphone is streamed to your own PC (microphone permission is asked on first use).
- Game audio keeps playing, and the stream stays connected, when you take the headset off (switch "Stream audio when headset removed", on by default, up to 30 minutes).
- Joy-Con controllers through hand tracking (default OFF, toggle to enable).
- Video stream sharpening and a field of view set by the AirFrameXR streamer, for a sharper picture.
- Performance and stability improvements.
